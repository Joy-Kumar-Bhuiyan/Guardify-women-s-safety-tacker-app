# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of identity, background, experience, or disability. Be respectful, protect survivor privacy, avoid victim-blaming, and never use project access to identify or track a person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, publishing private information, stalking, coercive location sharing, sexualized conduct, or abusing security reports. Maintainers may remove content, revoke access, or ban participants. Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled confidentially and with attention to the reporter's safety.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
